HowCome — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes what information the HowCome posting application ("the App"), operated by HowCome (howcomeofficial1@gmail.com), handles when it publishes videos to HowCome's own social media accounts, including the TikTok account @howcome217.

1. Who the App is for The App is a first-party tool used only by the owner of the HowCome channel. It is not offered to the public, and it does not collect information from viewers, followers, or any other third party.

2. Information the App accesses When the owner signs in with a platform (for example, TikTok Login Kit), the App receives: - Basic profile information for the owner's own account (such as username and display name; TikTok scope user.info.basic), used only to confirm the correct account is connected. - An access token that lets the App upload and publish the owner's videos on the owner's behalf (TikTok scopes video.upload and video.publish).

3. How information is used The information above is used only to (a) connect the owner's account, (b) upload and publish videos the owner has explicitly chosen, and (c) show the owner the publish status. It is never used for advertising, profiling, analytics about other people, or any other purpose.

4. Sharing No data received through platform APIs is sold, rented, or shared with third parties. Video files and captions are sent only to the platform the owner chose to publish on.

5. Storage and security Access tokens are stored securely and are never published or placed in public files. Video files remain the owner's property.

6. Retention and deletion Tokens are kept only while the App is in use and can be revoked by the owner at any time from the platform's connected-apps settings, which immediately ends the App's access. To request deletion of any stored data, contact howcomeofficial1@gmail.com and it will be deleted within 30 days.

7. Changes This policy may be updated; the current version will always be available at this URL.

8. Contact Privacy questions: howcomeofficial1@gmail.com
